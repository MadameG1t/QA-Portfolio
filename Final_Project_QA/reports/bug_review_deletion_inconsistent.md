GroceryMate – Star Rating System
Product page: Oranges

Steps to Reproduce
	1.	Log in as a user who has purchased the product.
	2.	Add a review (select stars + enter text).
	3.	Open the three-dots menu.
	4.	Click Delete.
	5.	Confirm the browser popup.
	6.	Refresh the product page.

Expected Result
	•	Review is removed.
	•	“Add a comment” form becomes visible again.
	•	No restriction message appears.

Actual Result
	•	After deletion and page refresh,
	•	The message “You have already reviewed this product.” still appears.
	•	The review appears not to be deleted.
	•	Repeating deletion sometimes resolves the issue.

Severity

Medium – affects user ability to manage their own reviews.
